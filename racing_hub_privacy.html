Privacy Policy

App: Racing Hub: Idle Tycoon

Developer: Mandg Studio

Last updated: August 2, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Racing Hub: Idle Tycoon ("the App") handles information. We aim to collect only what is needed to run the game, improve performance, show ads, and provide leaderboard features.

2. Information We Collect

  • Local game data: Progress, settings, and preferences stored on your device.
  • Anonymous account data: An anonymous Firebase user ID used for leaderboard features.
  • Leaderboard data: Display name and related score/ranking information.
  • Analytics data: App usage events via Firebase Analytics (for example screen views and gameplay events).
  • Advertising data: Advertising identifiers and ad interaction data via Google AdMob (for rewarded ads).
  • Purchase-related data: If/when in-app purchases are enabled, Google Play processes payment information. We do not receive your full payment card details.

We do not require you to create a traditional email/password account to play.

3. How We Use Information

  • To save and restore your game progress
  • To operate leaderboards
  • To understand app performance and improve gameplay
  • To show rewarded advertisements
  • To process in-app purchases (when available)

4. Third-Party Services

The App may use:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Analytics)
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play services / billing

These services have their own privacy policies. Please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob & privacy
  • Firebase Privacy

5. Data Storage and Retention

Local progress is stored on your device. Cloud-related data (such as anonymous IDs and leaderboard entries) is stored with Firebase and retained while needed for App functionality or as required by applicable law.

6. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your Choices

  • You can reset/clear app data from your device settings.
  • You can limit ad personalization in your device/Google settings.
  • You can uninstall the App at any time.
